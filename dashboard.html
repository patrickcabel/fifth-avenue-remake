WORKSPACE / RECOVERY

Local backups

Automatic hourly copies of requests, settings, and the staff account. The latest 168 regular snapshots are kept in the project’s backups folder outside Docker’s data volume.

Recent snapshots

These copies contain customer information and password hashes. Keep the folder private. They are on this computer, so copy them elsewhere for protection against a disk failure. Webhook secrets in .env are not included.

Restoring requires stopping the backend and running the documented restore command. Current data is saved to a safety copy first.

FRONT OF HOUSE / BOOKING RULES

Reservation settings

Table duration: 2 hours. Confirmed reservations are automatically cancelled if not marked arrived within 15 minutes. Check guests in promptly.

Reservation service hours

Default: Mon–Fri 11 AM–9 PM; Sat–Sun noon–9 PM. Latest two-hour reservation starts at 7 PM.

The entire two-hour booking must finish before closing. Leave unknown days unconfigured. These are same-day windows; overnight service is not supported yet.

Capacity counts confirmed and arrived bookings during their two-hour windows. Pending requests do not hold tables. Walk-ins are protected by the table allowance rather than tracked individually.
