Our Story

A restaurant shaped by hospitality and craft.

Fifth Avenue Sushi was built around a simple idea: serve impeccable Japanese food with the warmth and care of a neighbourhood favourite.

Rooted in authenticity.

Our story begins with a love for authentic Japanese dining and a belief that every guest deserves a full, memorable experience — not just a meal.

From the careful selection of ingredients to the precision of preparation, every step reflects a respect for tradition, quality, and hospitality.

In downtown Calgary, Fifth Avenue Sushi brings together refined technique, welcoming service, and a warm atmosphere that invites guests to linger, share, and enjoy.

The Experience

Welcoming, elevated, personal.

We believe great dining should feel both elevated and effortless — deeply flavourful, beautifully presented, and genuinely hospitable.

Our Journey

The values behind every dish.

Freshness first

We honour the integrity of every ingredient and prepare each order with attention to quality from start to finish.

Technique and care

Our kitchen combines time-honoured Japanese practices with an eye for modern dining refinement.

Guest experience

Whether it’s a quick lunch or an evening gathering, we create a space where comfort and excellence coexist.
