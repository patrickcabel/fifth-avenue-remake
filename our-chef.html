Meet Our Executive Chef

Chef Varamon Prua

More than 18 years of authentic Japanese cuisine, shaped by discipline, curiosity, and an unwavering commitment to freshness.

Crafted with intention.

Fifth Avenue Sushi is proud to welcome Chef Varamon Prua as our Executive Chef. A passionate sushi chef and Japanese cuisine enthusiast, Chef Prua has been perfecting his craft for more than 18 years.

His dedication to authentic sushi preparation and exceptional Japanese cuisine is reflected in every dish he creates, with the goal of providing every guest with a complete and memorable Japanese dining experience.

Chef Prua believes that mastering the art of sushi is a lifelong journey. He continually studies traditional Japanese techniques while exploring new culinary trends from around the world, always striving to refine his skills and elevate the dining experience.

Philosophy

Nothing beats freshness.

Every sushi order is prepared fresh, only after an order is placed — nothing is pre-made — ensuring the highest quality, flavor, and presentation.

Experience

18+

Years of precision, training, and dedication to authentic Japanese dining.

A Lifelong Craft

Tradition, learning, and refinement.

Technique first

Chef Prua studies the fundamentals of traditional Japanese gastronomy to build a strong foundation in precision and balance.

Curiosity always

He keeps exploring global culinary influences, searching for ideas that elevate flavour, texture, and creativity without losing authenticity.

Hospitality at heart

His work is not only about the plate — it is about the guest experience, from the first welcome to the final bite.
