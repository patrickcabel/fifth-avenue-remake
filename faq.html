FAQ

Everything you need to know before your visit.

We aim to make the experience as seamless and enjoyable as possible, whether you are joining us for lunch, dinner, or a special occasion.

Do you accept reservations?

Yes. Reservations are recommended, especially during peak lunch and dinner times. You can reserve online through our reservation page.

Is the sushi prepared fresh to order?

Yes. Our philosophy is centered on freshness, and each sushi order is prepared fresh after it is placed to ensure quality and flavour.

Are there menu options for dietary preferences?

We can guide guests toward dishes that suit their preferences. Please contact us in advance or speak with the team when you arrive.

Do you offer a bar and sake menu?

Yes. We offer a curated selection of drinks and sake designed to complement our dishes and dining experience.

Is the restaurant suitable for special occasions?

Absolutely. Many guests choose Fifth Avenue Sushi for intimate dinners, celebrations, and memorable evenings with friends and family.

Where is Fifth Avenue Sushi located?

We are located at 118A 5th Ave SE in downtown Calgary, Alberta.
